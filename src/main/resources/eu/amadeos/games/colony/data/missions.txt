# What each mission gives the settlement and what it asks of it. Times in seconds.
#
#   map       the map in the folder maps, made in Tiled (see the README)
#   start     the goods in the storehouse at the start, as pairs of good and count
#   settlers  how many settlers live in the storehouse at the start
#   goal      how many settlers the settlement must have to win
#   goods     goods the storehouses must hold at the same time, as pairs (none if not given)
#   years     the goal must be reached before this many years are over (0: no limit)
#   seasons   the lengths of spring, summer, autumn and winter
#   fire      yes if houses catch fire
#   wolves    yes if wolves come from their dens in autumn and winter
